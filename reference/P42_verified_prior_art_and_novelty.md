## Scientific judgment
The 2003 parity prior art is a significant correction to the programme's novelty framing. P42's locally finite analytic argument is mathematically sound given the P38 witness, but no publication-level priority claim is established. The next work should shift toward a genuinely independent inverse theorem, sharp quantitative threshold, or duality-quotient invariant not already implicit in the parity literature.
