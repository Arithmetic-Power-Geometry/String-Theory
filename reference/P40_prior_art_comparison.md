## Novelty evaluation
Standard parity reversal and the distinction between total energy and left/right conformal weights are not new. Higher-genus distinction of genus-one-isospectral code CFTs is prior art. The P40 exact rational open-family certificate and 1/90 perturbation radius are specific quantitative contributions, but a full priority/novelty claim requires further searching and independent review. No assertion that P40 supersedes prior work is justified.
