# P08 — Higher-genus obstruction to an APG spectral breakthrough

Date: 2026-10-24
Verdict: rigorous finite-model observation; existing string-theory prior art blocks a novelty claim.

## Exact finite-model theorem
Let S be a finite subset of Z and let the one-variable theta-like energy polynomial be T_S(x)=sum_(a in S) x^(a^2). Define additive interaction incidence C(S)=#{(a,b,c) in S^3: a+b=c} (ordered triples; repeated elements permitted).

Take A={0,1,2} and B={0,1,-2}. Then T_A=T_B=1+x+x^4, but C(A)=6 and C(B)=5. Proof: count ordered additive triples directly. This demonstrates that a finite energy multiset does not determine additive incidence. It is not a result about complete Narain lattices or a physically consistent CFT.

## Physical interpretation and strict limitation
In lattice CFT, momentum conservation imposes charge-addition selection rules on vertex-operator correlators. However, all charges form an entire lattice, and the physical correlators, cocycles and modular constraints cannot be modeled by an arbitrary finite subset without further justification. This toy observation is not a counterexample to completeness of any full CFT invariant.

## Decisive prior art
Henriksson, Kakkar and McPeak, 'Narain CFTs and quantum codes at higher genus,' JHEP 04 (2023) 011, DOI 10.1007/JHEP04(2023)011, already exhibit isospectral genus-one code CFTs distinguished at genus two. The general proposition that higher-genus data can separate theories with the same torus partition function is thus known.

Other relevant work: 'Harmonic analysis of 2d CFT partition functions,' JHEP 09 (2021) 174, DOI 10.1007/JHEP09(2021)174; Ando, Kawabata and Nishioka, 'Gauging Z_N symmetries of Narain CFTs,' JHEP 02 (2026) 058, DOI 10.1007/JHEP02(2026)058.

## Research direction beyond existing work
A publishable APG theorem would need a new sharp, duality-invariant bound on higher-genus interaction-sensitive quantities using APG's exponent-deformation geometry, with a nontrivial equality or obstruction classification not implied by theta-series/weight-enumerator identities. First prove it for a complete modular-invariant family, then independently compare with higher-genus code CFT results. Do not claim a breakthrough from the finite subset example.

## Outcome
P08 identifies a mathematically sound finite separation and a direct prior-art collision. Novelty verdict NO-GO for the claim 'genus-two distinguishes genus-one-isospectral Narain theories'. No string-theory breakthrough established.
