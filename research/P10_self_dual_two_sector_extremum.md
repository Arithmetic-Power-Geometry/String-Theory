# P10 — Sharp APG two-sector extremum at self-dual circle radius
Date: 2026-10-24. Status: exact theorem for a deliberately restricted two-sector construction; NOT a string-theory breakthrough.

Take alpha'=1, R>0, and the unit momentum/winding energy contributions E_m=R^-2, E_w=R^2. Define u=E_m/(E_m+E_w)=1/(1+R^4), v=E_w/(E_m+E_w)=R^4/(1+R^4). For q>1 set D_(2q)(R)=1-u^q-v^q.

THEOREM P10-T1: D_(2q)(R)=D_(2q)(1/R), and D_(2q)(R)<=1-2^(1-q), with equality if and only if R=1. Proof: inversion exchanges u and v. Strict convexity of x^q gives u^q+v^q>=2^(1-q), with equality only when u=v=1/2, equivalent to R=1.

COROLLARY P10-C1: D_4(R)=2R^4/(1+R^4)^2, uniquely maximized at R=1 with value 1/2.

Scope: the normalized weights are energy contributions of two selected charge sectors, NOT their Gibbs probabilities and NOT the full Narain partition function. The maximum is a consequence of elementary convexity and known self-dual symmetry, so is not a novel result in string theory. Other radii and string normalization conventions rescale the self-dual radius accordingly.
