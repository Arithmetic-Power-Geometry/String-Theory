# P22 — Sharp obstruction to a fixed four-probe Narain reconstruction protocol

Date: 2026-10-24. Mathematical result: minimality of P21's five probes *within the protocol of three specified pure-momentum measurements plus one arbitrary fixed charge-energy measurement*.

Let G be any positive-definite real symmetric 2x2 matrix, A=G^-1, and B=b J with J=[[0,1],[-1,0]]. E_b(m,n)=(m+bJn)^T A(m+bJn)+n^T G n.

P22-T1. The three energies E(e1), E(e2), E(e1+e2) determine A and G, but adding one fixed arbitrary charge energy E_b(m,n) cannot identify b globally on R. If n=0 the fourth measurement is independent of b. Otherwise E_b(m,n)=alpha*b^2+beta*b+gamma with alpha=(Jn)^T A (Jn)>0 and beta=2m^T A(Jn). Reflection b'=-beta/alpha-b gives E_b'(m,n)=E_b(m,n). For every b other than -beta/(2alpha), b' differs from b. Pure-momentum energies do not depend on b. Hence the four-measurement map is not injective. The P21 five-probe reconstruction is sufficient and minimal within this restricted protocol.

Explicit witness: G=I, fourth v=e4=(0,0,0,1). For b=1/4 and b'=-1/4, all four specified energies agree, while the mixed fifth probe e1+e4 distinguishes them.

LIMITS: Does not show that every four-probe protocol is insufficient; does not show b and b' represent distinct physical theories modulo O(2,2;Z). It is elementary quadratic measurement ambiguity, and prior-art novelty is not established.

P23: Study arbitrary four-probe families, quotient by physical T-duality, and derive a duality-invariant certificate or a rigorous impossibility theorem. Avoid asserting breakthrough without independent prior-art audit.
