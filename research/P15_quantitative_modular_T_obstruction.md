# P15 — Quantitative modular-T obstruction for formal phase deformations

Date: 2026-10-24
Research verdict: rigorous quantitative bound; novelty relative to literature not established; no string-theory breakthrough claimed.

## Model
For R,t>0, real lambda, and tau=tau1+i*tau2, define
Theta_(lambda,R)(tau)=sum_(m,n in Z) exp[-pi*tau2*(m²/R²+n²R²)] exp[2*pi*i*lambda*tau1*m*n].
The sum converges absolutely for every tau2>0. It is a formal phase-deformed zero-mode theta series, not a complete CFT partition function.

Define the real T-discrepancy A_lambda(R,t)=Re[Theta_(lambda,R)(it)-Theta_(lambda,R)(1+it)].

## Theorem P15-T1 (quantitative obstruction)
For all R,t>0 and real lambda,
A_lambda(R,t) >= 4 exp[-pi*t*(R²+R^-2)] (1-cos(2*pi*lambda)).
In particular A_lambda(R,t)>0 for noninteger lambda, so T invariance is impossible.

Proof: Expand the real difference as
sum_(m,n in Z) exp[-pi*t*(m²/R²+n²R²)] * [1-cos(2*pi*lambda*m*n)].
Every term is nonnegative. The four states (m,n)=(+/-1,+/-1) each contribute exp[-pi*t*(R²+R^-2)] * [1-cos(2*pi*lambda)]. Discarding the remaining nonnegative terms proves the inequality. For noninteger lambda the factor 1-cos(2*pi*lambda)>0. If lambda is integer, all terms vanish.

## Corollary P15-C1
For every fixed R,t>0, A_lambda(R,t)=0 iff lambda is an integer. Thus the continuous phase-only deformation has an explicit, nonzero modular-T obstruction at each noninteger coupling.

## Caution and prior art
This is a quantitative specialization of known spin-integrality/level-matching constraints. It does not rule out exactly marginal compact-boson radius deformations, which alter the background consistently. Full modular invariance also requires S and the oscillator sector; this theorem concerns a specific formal phase deformation. The lower bound is not asserted optimal for all lambda,R,t. No claim of new physics.
