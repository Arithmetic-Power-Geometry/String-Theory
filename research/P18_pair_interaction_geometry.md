# P18 — Pair-Interaction Geometry: candidate APG refinement
Date 2026-10-24. For v=(m,n), w=(m',n') in Z^4 define L(v,w)=m·n'+m'·n. With positive-definite Narain energy E_(G,B)(v), define P(t,s,z)=sum_(v,w) exp(-tE(v)-sE(w)+izL(v,w)), t,s>0, z real.

T1 Absolute convergence: |P|<=Z(t)Z(s)<infinity.
T2 Integral B-shift covariance: B'=B+K, m'=m-Kn, K antisymmetric integral. Energy is preserved, and L(v',w')=L(v,w) since n^T K n'+n'^T K n=0. Therefore P is preserved by the bijection on ordered lattice pairs. Finite fixed boxes need not be invariant: transform the actual charge subset.
T3 Information-loss example: G=I, B=0; v=(1,0,0,0), w=(0,0,1,0), u=(0,1,0,0). E(v)=E(w)=E(u)=1, but L(v,w)=1 and L(v,u)=0. Pairwise pairing is not determined by the two individual energies. This does not prove inequivalent full CFTs share the same genus-one partition function.

Novelty: L is the standard Narain bilinear pairing. P may be a specialization of established genus-two or flavored theta functions. No claim of a new physical invariant, full Sp(4,Z) modular covariance, or a string-theory breakthrough.
P19 decisive gates: derive exact relation to Siegel–Narain genus-two theta; test full modular transformation, oscillator factors and sewing constraints; construct or rule out inequivalent isospectral Narain CFT examples; certify infinite tails and compare with prior art.
