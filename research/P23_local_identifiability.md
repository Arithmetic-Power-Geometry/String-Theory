# P23 — Four-probe local identifiability and global ambiguity

Date: 2026-10-24.

For G=[[a,d],[d,c]] positive definite and B=[[0,b],[-b,0]], let F(G,b)=(E(e1),E(e2),E(e1+e2),E(e3)), with charge ordering (m1,m2,n1,n2). Exact symbolic differentiation gives det D_(a,c,d,b) F = -4*a*b/(a*c-d^2)^4. Thus the determinant is nonzero whenever b != 0, and the inverse function theorem gives a local smooth inverse at every such background. This does not imply global injectivity: after the first three momentum measurements determine G, the fourth depends quadratically on b, giving a reflection ambiguity.

A general dimension lower bound: No continuous map from the open four-dimensional parameter domain {(a,c,d,b):a>0,c>0,ac>d^2} into R^k for k<4 is injective, by invariance of domain applied after embedding R^k into R^4. Thus any globally injective fixed continuous real-valued probe family needs at least four probes. Combining P21 and P22/P23 yields 4 <= N_min <= 5 for fixed labelled charge-energy probe families, without resolving whether four globally suffice.

The local four-probe result follows from classical inverse function theory, not a demonstrated new physical effect. Modulo O(2,2;Z), global classification remains open within this programme. Prior-art novelty unverified.
