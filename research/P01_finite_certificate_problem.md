# P01 — Finite spectral-interaction certificates

## Precise model
Take a closed connected Riemannian manifold M with the nonnegative scalar Laplace–Beltrami operator Δ. Let λ₀=0<λ₁≤… with eigenfunctions φᵢ orthonormal in L²(M).

Given a fixed spectral window consisting of complete eigenspaces, define the tensor C_ijk=∫_M φᵢ φⱼ φ_k dvol. Its arrays depend on eigenbases; compare only under block-orthogonal transformations within repeated eigenspaces. Define a certificate as a *basis-invariant* polynomial contraction (or a minimization over such transformations) of a finite tensor-window that separates two models.

## Questions, not claimed theorems
Q1: Is there a restricted family of isospectral nonisometric compactification candidates distinguished by fewer explicitly bounded basis-invariant coupling measurements than previously known?
Q2: Can a quantitative separation margin be established under measurement errors?
Q3: Do the models actually satisfy relevant string-theoretic conditions and avoid being physically dual?

## Strong negative control
If comparison is only of full eigenvalue multisets, classical counterexamples already show failure of geometric identification. If full indexed triple-product multiplication structure is used, Schaefer 2024 poses direct prior art. A valid novelty result must lie between these extremes and exceed known finite spectral reconstruction results.

## Physical validity checklist
Do not call an arbitrary graph, modular curve, or compact Riemannian manifold a string vacuum. Specify dimensions, worldsheet/supergravity approximation, geometry, fluxes/bundles, anomaly/tadpole cancellation as applicable, moduli stabilization assumptions, and gauge/duality invariance before physical claims.
