# E002: APG spectral-energy stress test (2026-10-24)

On a circle with normalized measure, consider rho_k(x)=1+epsilon cos(kx), where 0<epsilon<=1 and k is a positive integer. The mean-zero source is F_k=epsilon cos(kx). The Poisson solution for the positive Laplacian is u_k=epsilon cos(kx)/k^2.

Exact integration yields source squared norm epsilon^2/2 and Dirichlet energy epsilon^2/(2k^2). The first positive Laplace eigenvalue is 1. Thus the familiar spectral-gap inequality is satisfied, with equality for k=1.

With fixed external coordinate weights W, all examples have the same Shannon entropy and source norm, while their energies differ. This is an elementary example of source spectral-distribution dependence. It is NOT a new theorem and NOT a contradiction of APG VIII or X. The constructed sources have not been shown admissible under the APG IX source map.
